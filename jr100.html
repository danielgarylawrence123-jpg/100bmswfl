⬥ Our Mission

Making the 100 Difference.

100 Black Men of Southwest Florida Bridge Builders Mentoring Program works to empower our Bridge Builders to make positive life choices that enable them to maximize their potential.

The Jr. 100 Bridge Builders Leadership Academy is the cornerstone of how we are making The 100 DIFFERENCE — pairing young men in grades 6–12 with professional mentors who give their time, guidance, and lived experience.

⬥ Our Framework

Five Program Keys.

01

Develop and Promote Good Character

Building integrity, accountability, and values that guide our young men through every stage of life.

02

Promote Literacy

Strengthening reading, writing, and critical thinking as the foundation for lifelong learning and opportunity.

03

Academic Excellence & Post-High Readiness

Providing tutoring, college readiness support, and guidance through the transition beyond high school.

04

Career Pathways & Internships

Crafting field-specific career pathways with professional mentors and hands-on internship and apprenticeship opportunities.

05

Cultural Enrichment

Enhancing cultural pride, community connection, and a sense of identity and belonging in every young man we serve.
